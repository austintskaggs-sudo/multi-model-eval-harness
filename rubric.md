# Scoring rubric

Score every run with the same rubric, after the task is complete.
Each criterion is 0, 1, or 2. Total is out of 10.

## Criteria

### Accuracy (0-2)
- 2: All key facts, calculations, and conclusions are correct.
- 1: Mostly correct, with one or two small errors.
- 0: A key fact or conclusion is wrong, or an important detail is invented.

### Completeness (0-2)
- 2: Every part of the task is addressed.
- 1: Most parts are addressed, but one part is thin or missing.
- 0: A major part of the task is skipped.

### Reasoning / tool steps (0-2)
- 2: Steps are in a sensible order, and intermediate results are shown or explained.
- 1: Steps are mostly sensible, but one step is out of order or unexplained.
- 0: Steps are missing, confused, or jump straight to an unsupported answer.

### Clarity (0-2)
- 2: Easy to read, well structured, and easy to check.
- 1: Readable, but messy or harder to check in places.
- 0: Hard to follow or hard to verify.

### Format following (0-2)
- 2: Follows the requested format exactly (headings, tables, sections).
- 1: Mostly follows it, with a small format miss.
- 0: Ignores the requested format.

## Notes to record for every run

- Model label shown by the service
- Date
- Time taken, roughly, in minutes
- Any failure mode: missed step, wrong calculation, hallucinated detail, format ignored, or other
